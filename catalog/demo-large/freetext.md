This is the data of an online shop for outdoor sports gear: its own web shop, its sales on two marketplaces, its advertising on five channels, and its web analytics.

- products, campaigns, customers: the product catalogue, all ad campaigns across channels, and the shop's customers.
- shop_orders, shop_order_items, shop_returns: every web-shop order, the products in it, and returned items.
- shop_sales_daily, shop_sales_weekly, shop_sales_monthly: web-shop orders and revenue per day, week and month.
- shop_sales_net_of_returns_daily: web-shop revenue per day after refunds.
- shop_sales_daily_v1: the old daily sales table, no longer maintained.
- shop_product_sales_daily: web-shop units and revenue per day and product.
- shop_inventory_daily: stock per product at the end of each day.
- marketplace_amazon_sales_daily, marketplace_otto_sales_daily: sales on Amazon and on Otto per day and product.
- marketplace_sales_daily: both marketplaces per day.
- marketplace_amazon_fees_monthly: fees Amazon charged per month.
- ads_google_search_campaign_daily, ads_google_shopping_product_daily, ads_meta_campaign_daily, ads_microsoft_campaign_daily, ads_amazon_sp_campaign_daily: daily ad performance per channel.
- ads_all_channels_daily, ads_all_channels_weekly, ads_all_channels_monthly: all ad channels together per day, week and month.
- ads_all_channels_daily_v1: the old cross-channel spend table, no longer maintained.
- marketing_budget_monthly: planned ad budget per month and channel.
- web_sessions_daily: website sessions per day and channel group.
- web_conversions_last_click_daily, web_conversions_first_click_daily, web_conversions_data_driven_daily: web-shop purchases per day and channel group under three attribution models.
- web_funnel_daily, web_landing_pages_daily, web_site_search_weekly: the purchase funnel per device, landing pages, and site-search terms.
- email_sends_daily: email sends, opens and clicks per campaign.
