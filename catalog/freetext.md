This is the data of an online shop for outdoor sports gear and its advertising.

- ads_campaign_daily: daily advertising performance per campaign — impressions, clicks, cost, and the sales and orders attributed to the ads.
- campaigns: the advertising campaigns with their name, channel (search or display) and status.
- products: the shop's product catalogue with category and list price.
- shop_orders_daily: orders from the online shop per day and product — units ordered, units returned and revenue.
- web_sessions_daily: website traffic per day and marketing channel — sessions, engaged sessions and purchases.
